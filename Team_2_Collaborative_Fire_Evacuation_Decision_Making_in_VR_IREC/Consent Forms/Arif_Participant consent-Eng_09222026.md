## Introduction

You are invited to take part in a research study about **collaborative decision-making in Virtual Reality (VR)**. Before you decide whether to participate, please read the information below. You may ask any questions you have before signing this form. **Participation is entirely voluntary.**

---

## Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to understand how different ways of presenting incomplete, uncertain, or conflicting information affect teamwork and decision-making in a shared VR emergency scenario. **Two participants** work together but receive different information. The study compares private role-specific information, continuously shared information, and adaptive uncertainty cues that appear only when a relevant conflict or missing piece of evidence is detected. **The study is a research prototype and is not professional emergency-response training.**

---

## What Will I Be Asked to Do?

You will participate as part of a two-person team. One person will be assigned the role of a **Control-Room Analyst** and the other will be an **On-Site Responder**. The Analyst reviews virtual maps, alarms, sensor readings, and equipment information. The Responder uses a Meta Quest 2 headset to inspect rooms, hazards, workers, routes, and equipment in the virtual facility. You and your partner will communicate naturally and work together to make shared decisions.

- Complete a brief consent and background stage, including previous VR experience.
- Receive a short tutorial and complete one practice trial so you are familiar with the VR controls and study tasks.
- Complete matched emergency scenarios under three information-sharing conditions: private, always-shared, and adaptive.
- Work with your partner to decide the likely emergency source, an evacuation route, an equipment action, and rescue priority.
- Complete short questionnaires about workload, trust, confidence, shared awareness, usability, and information overload, and provide brief comments about the interface.

The VR system will automatically record task-related information such as decisions, response times, evidence inspections, room visits, teleports, revisits, and other navigation events. **Audio and video of participants will not be recorded**.

---

## Risks and Possible Discomfort

This study is considered minimal risk. **You may experience** mild and temporary effects commonly associated with VR use, such as eye strain, dizziness, slight motion sickness, or discomfort from wearing the headset. The fictional emergency scenario may also cause minor temporary stress. To reduce these risks, the study uses teleportation and snap turning rather than continuous artificial movement, the scenarios are non-graphic, and breaks are available. **You may pause or stop** the session **immediately at any time**. If you feel unwell, the headset will be removed, and the session will be stopped. Individuals for whom VR use may be unsafe will be excluded during screening.

---

## Benefits

You may benefit from gaining hands-on experience with a collaborative VR system and from learning how information sharing, uncertainty, and communication can influence team decisions. **There is no guarantee of direct personal benefit**. The study may contribute to the design of future collaborative VR systems that support shared awareness and decision-making without overwhelming users with unnecessary information.

---

## Compensation

**No payment** or other **tangible compensation** is planned for participation. A summary of the research results will be available after the study is completed and may be requested from the research team.

## Confidentiality and Privacy

Your identity will be protected as far as reasonably possible. Your name and contact information will be used only for recruitment, scheduling, and consent. Each participant will receive a participant code (for example, P01) and each pair will receive a pair code (for example, Pair01). These codes, rather than names, will be used in VR performance logs, questionnaires, and analysis files.

Electronic research data will be stored on a password-protected research computer at Nazarbayev University. Signed consent forms and any document linking names to study codes will be stored separately from the research dataset in a locked cabinet. Access will be limited to the research team. Results will be reported only in aggregate or de-identified form. No student IDs, audio recordings, video recordings, or identifiable biometric data will be included in the research dataset. Although reasonable safeguards will be used, complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

---

## Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

**Participation is strictly voluntary**. You may refuse to participate or withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without penalty or negative consequences. Participation or non-participation will not affect your grades, academic standing, employment, university services, or relationship with Nazarbayev University or the researchers.

If you withdraw during a paired VR trial, the current collaborative trial will stop because it requires two participants. You may ask for data collected before withdrawal to be excluded while it can still be linked to your participant code. Once data has been fully de-identified and combined into aggregate results, it may no longer be possible to identify and remove an individual record.
