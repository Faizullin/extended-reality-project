# Participants Needed for a VR Fire-Evacuation Research Study

We are conducting a research study at Nazarbayev University on collaborative decision-making during simulated fire evacuation in Virtual Reality. Participants will work in pairs using Meta Quest 2 headsets. One participant will act as a Control-Room Analyst with access to a map and sensor information, while the second participant will act as an On-Site Responder who navigates the virtual environment and identifies a safe evacuation route with the Analyst's guidance. Audio communication during the task will be recorded for research purposes.

Participation will take approximately 50-60 minutes. Participants must be 18 years or older and able to understand study instructions in English. Participation is completely voluntary, and participants may pause or stop at any time, including if they experience VR-related discomfort.

If you are interested in participating or would like more information, please contact the research team at [zhandaulet.kuan@nu.edu.kz](mailto:zhandaulet.kuan@nu.edu.kz).
